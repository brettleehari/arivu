Arivu — Support

Arivu is a language model that runs entirely on your phone. There is no account, no server and no network connection, which shapes most of the answers below.

Contact

Email arivu.ai.org@gmail.com. One person reads it, so a reply may take a few days.

Reporting a bad reply

Every reply in the app has a Report button beneath it. It opens your own email app with that reply attached, and nothing is sent until you press send — you can read and edit it first. That is the most useful way to report a wrong or harmful answer, because it tells us exactly what was said.

Reported emails are kept for 90 days and then deleted.

Things that are working as intended

  • The first launch is slow. The app is loading a model of about a gigabyte from its own storage. Later launches are quicker.
  • Replies are slower than a cloud assistant. This is a small model running on a phone battery, not a data centre.
  • It gets facts wrong. It is good at working with text you give it — rewriting, shortening, explaining, summarising, translating. It is unreliable about facts, dates, scores and anything recent, and it has no way to look anything up. Check anything that matters.
  • It forgets the start of a long conversation. It can hold about 2000 tokens at once. When older messages fall out of range the app draws a line saying so, rather than losing them silently.

Deleting your data

Conversations are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and are excluded from device backups. Swipe a conversation in the list, or tap Edit, to delete it. Deleting the app deletes everything — there is no copy anywhere else, because there is nowhere else.

It will not start

Arivu needs roughly 3.5 GB of memory and iOS 17 or later. If your device is below that, the app says so plainly on first launch and stops, rather than starting and failing later.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. The short version: nothing you type leaves your phone, because the app contains no networking code at all.
